    How this works

    One board, two prompts

    Red and Yellow alternate. On each turn the app sends the board and the legal columns to that model and asks for one digit. A reply that is not a legal column falls back to the local bot, and the log says so.

    Your key, your Pollen

    The key stays in your browser and is sent only to gen.pollinations.ai. There is no backend and no shared key. Get one at enter.pollinations.ai/keys.

    No-key demo

    The demo runs two local heuristic players. It never calls a model and never spends Pollen, so you can watch a whole game without connecting anything.
